About Ray

Built on Integrity.
Driven by Results.

Real estate is the only career Ray Morandi has ever known. Born and raised in Orland Park, Ray got his start in the business at just 19 years old, learning the ropes alongside his father — a racehorse veterinarian who invested in real estate. That early foundation instilled in him a work ethic and a standard of service that has defined his career ever since.

As the Managing Broker and Owner of Morandi Properties Inc., Ray serves buyers and sellers throughout the southwest suburbs of Chicago — from first-time homebuyers to high-end sellers, new construction, and bank-owned properties. His willingness to take on listings that were poorly marketed, approach them correctly, and get them sold has earned him a reputation that speaks for itself.

What sets Ray apart is a commitment to constant communication, clean honesty, and innovative marketing. Every listing, regardless of price point, receives professional photography, drone footage, 3D walkthroughs, and a video marketing strategy. He was an early adopter of video marketing in the area, launching Morandi Properties Videos — engaging, MTV Cribs-inspired walkthroughs that bring listings to life.

With over 25 years in the business, Ray's client base is built almost entirely on referrals — a fact he credits not to a CRM or automated marketing, but to the quality of every client experience. When people trust you enough to send their friends and family, you know you're doing something right.
